## Research Collaborations


### La Trobe University, Australia · Reliable Online Decisions and Heterogeneous Network Optimization

*Research collaborator: [Di Wu](https://diwu.work/) · 2025 – Present · Ongoing research collaboration*

- **Model-Assisted Decision-Making under Uncertainty:** Investigated reliable decision-making in complex systems where predictive and generative models, including large-model-assisted approaches, support the exploration of alternative actions. Studied how imperfect information, estimation uncertainty, and available evidence influence decision quality, with an emphasis on robust evaluation and dependable decision support.

- **Adaptive Learning with Imperfect Feedback:** Studied online learning and continual adaptation in dynamic networked environments with delayed observations, changing data distributions, and limited computational resources. Explored resource-aware learning strategies and evaluation methods to understand how feedback quality, system variability, and operational constraints affect the reliability and effectiveness of adaptive models.

- **Online Network Optimization and Secure Communication:** Investigated online routing, resource allocation, and sequential optimization in networked systems with evolving operating conditions and interdependent decisions. In parallel, studied reliable and cost-efficient key delivery in heterogeneous space–air–ground–sea quantum key distribution (QKD) networks, focusing on communication reliability, service requirements, and efficient resource coordination. Related QKD research was **submitted to IEEE TCOM**.

*Related collaborative research includes three manuscripts under review at ICLR 2027, spanning reliable decision-making, adaptive learning, and online optimization. The QKD-network study is a separate communication-systems project.*


### A*STAR, Singapore · Federated Intelligence and Cooperative Maritime Sensing

*Research collaborator: [Xiaojuan Zhang](https://research.a-star.edu.sg/researcher/xiao-juan-zhang/) · 2025 – Present · Ongoing research collaboration*

- **Federated Decision-Making for UAV–USV Cooperation:** Investigated cooperative sensing and resource-aware control in maritime UAV–USV networks under coupled communication, energy, and task-queue constraints. Explored Federated PPO and UAV-assisted policy aggregation for decentralized decision-making, balancing sensing quality, information exchange, and operational efficiency. Related work was **submitted to IEEE TVT**.

- **Robust Hierarchical Federated Sensing:** Investigated collaborative perception across heterogeneous maritime edge devices under non-IID data, unreliable wireless updates, and potentially anomalous client contributions. Explored hierarchical federated fusion with update-quality screening, robust aggregation, and soft synchronization to improve the reliability of distributed model coordination. Related work is **under review at IEEE IoTJ**.

- **Multi-Node Cooperative Sensing in Maritime Networks:** Studied distributed perception, communication-efficient information exchange, and cross-platform coordination in heterogeneous maritime IoT systems. Examined the integration of sensing, communication, and cooperative intelligence, identifying reliability, resource allocation, and scalability challenges for practical maritime applications. Related review work is **under review at IEEE Sensors Journal**.
