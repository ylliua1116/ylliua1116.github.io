The research program follows one central question: **how can intelligent networked systems make reliable decisions when communication and feedback are imperfect?**

### Causal Decision Learning & Online Optimization

How can causal effects be evaluated and translated into reliable sequential decisions under interference and incomplete feedback?

- Causal identification and off-policy evaluation under interference
- Doubly robust estimation, uncertainty and regret
- Adaptive online decisions and deployment reliability

### LLM Agents & Causal World Models

How can LLM-generated action proposals be evaluated through causal world models before they affect real systems?

- LLM-generated candidate actions and causal critics
- Dynamic causal world models and counterfactual value
- Regret-aware action selection

### Communication-Aware Cooperative Intelligence

How can agents communicate and collaborate when bandwidth, latency and shared environments constrain collective decisions?

- Decision-relevant semantic communication and reliable HARQ
- Network interference, heterogeneous coordination and delayed feedback
- Communication-efficient distributed and federated learning

### Heterogeneous Networks & SAG-S Deployment

Where can the methods be tested under real constraints, from UAV–USV systems to integrated space–air–ground–sea networks?

- UAV–USV collaboration and maritime edge offloading
- Low-altitude aerial sensing and heterogeneous edge systems
- Integrated space–air–ground–sea networks
