My work centers on **causally reliable decision-making for wireless networked intelligence**. I study how distributed agents learn, communicate, and act under interference, resource constraints, and imperfect feedback.

My research brings together **causal decision learning and online optimization**, **communication-aware cooperative intelligence**, and **heterogeneous wireless networks**, with applications including UAV–USV collaboration and maritime edge systems.

My academic background includes Master's-level study in Smart Ocean Technology at the **University of Macau** (2024–2026). My earlier training is in Communication Engineering. [Explore my research agenda →](/research/)
